# Prompt Set

## Shared direction

Create a single isolated website hero asset on a genuinely transparent background, composed for the right side of a wide enterprise webpage with generous transparent breathing space. Match the approved SAP Analytics Cloud pilot's premium realistic CGI product-visualization language: front three-quarter tabletop view, precise geometry, physically plausible matte polymer, anodized metal, coated paper and satin finishes, restrained soft contact shadows, and refined studio lighting. Use deep navy, cobalt blue, teal, white and graphite with only small restrained amber or coral accents. Keep it sophisticated and credible: not cute, not toy-like, not flat vector, not an icon collage, not neon and not excessively glassy. Exclude company logos, brand marks, letters, words, numbers, watermarks and readable UI.
