# Adobe Firefly Master Prompt Document — In2IT EBS Website

> **Production Guide & Copy-Paste Prompt Catalog (Updated v3.0 — Dynamic Collaboration & Rich Detailing)**  
> **Generation Engine**: Adobe Firefly (Image 3 / Generative AI)  
> **Target Format**: High-resolution PNG (Rendered on pure white studio background `#FFFFFF`, alpha-transparent cutout with natural ground contact shadow)  
> **Visual Art Direction (Based on Approved Industrial Reference)**:
> 1. **DYNAMIC COLLABORATION (FACING EACH OTHER)**: People are NOT turned with their backs to the camera. They interact naturally and dynamically, facing each other in dynamic 3/4 profiles, gesturing, scanning, and reviewing blueprints/consoles together.
> 2. **UNIDENTIFIABLE / NON-RECOGNIZABLE FACES**: Heads are angled and tilted downward toward their work, tablets, and equipment. Facial features are clean, generic, and stylized so that individual identity is completely anonymous and cannot be recognized.
> 3. **NATURAL & CONTEXTUAL HUMAN PRESENCE**: No rigid count constraint; human presence is tailored naturally to each scene (e.g., 2 focused specialists for an engineering rig, 2-3 consultants at an executive podium, or uncrewed hardware where appropriate).
> 4. **RICH INDUSTRIAL & TECHNICAL DETAILING**: Emphasize high-precision 3D components—modular battery trays, hydraulic cylinders, fiber-optic routing, structural trusses, ceramic insulators, electronic scanners, and unlabelled technical blueprint screens.
> 5. **ABSOLUTE EXCLUSIONS (NO TEXT, NO LOGOS, NO WATERMARKS)**: Strictly zero letters, words, numbers, or brand trademarks on any surface or screen. Screens display purely unlabelled geometric charts, wireframe diagrams, and visual waveforms.
> 6. **CLEAN STUDIO CUTOUT**: Pure white studio background (`#FFFFFF`) with crisp directional key light and soft, realistic contact drop shadows under feet and equipment for 1-click transparent PNG separation.

---

## Technical Parameters for Adobe Firefly

When generating in Adobe Firefly:
- **General Settings**:
  - **Content Type**: `Art` (or `Photo` with Style: High-End 3D Industrial Product Render / Octane Render / Keyshot).
  - **Aspect Ratio**: `16:9` (Widescreen) for hero banners; `1:1` (Square) for case study cards.
  - **Visual Intensity**: High detail, crisp geometric edge definition, and rich mechanical/architectural realism.
- **Negative Prompt / Exclusions** (Paste in Adobe Firefly's "Avoid" / Negative Prompt field):
  ```text
  text, typography, letters, words, font, watermark, logo, brand name, signature, label, numbers, identifiable celebrity face, photo portrait, messy background, cluttered backdrop, dark background, blurry edges
  ```
- **Lighting & Ground**:
  - Crisp studio softbox lighting with realistic soft ambient occlusion and contact drop shadows.
  - Solid pure white studio backdrop (`#FFFFFF`).
