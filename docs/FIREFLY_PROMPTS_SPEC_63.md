2. No people, no silhouettes, no buildings, no recognisable place, no logos.
3. Only the brand colours: deep navy, steel blue, light blue, pale blue, off-white field, one thin sand accent. Reject anything with other colours.
4. One simple object, lots of empty space, the object sitting right of centre.
5. Review all 63 together on one contact sheet before accepting any. Expect to redo about one in three.
6. Make image 1 first and keep it. Generate all of them in one sitting.
