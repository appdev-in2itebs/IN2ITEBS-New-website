# Firefly prompt sheet: content-specific page images

24 November 2026. Generated on Adobe Firefly Pro Plus. Each image is written from the real content of the page it sits on. Block 1 below is the Services section (21 pages). Blocks for the homepage, industries, insights, about, careers and contact follow once block 1 is approved.

## Rules

1. Content-specific: the image shows what that page is about.
2. Human rule (critical): if the image needs a human, include the person, but the face and identity must not be recognisable. The person is shown strictly from behind or at a three-quarter rear angle, head turned away or obscured by equipment, helmet or distance. Any result where a face can be recognised is rejected and regenerated.
3. Photorealistic, not cartoon or illustration. Every object keeps the real colour of its real material (steel is steel grey, glass is clear, copper is copper); no brand or special colour is imposed on any product.
4. No text, no fake labels, no logos, no brand names, no watermarks.
5. One look across the set: same camera, light and materials. Item S01 is approved first and then used as the Style reference for every other item.
6. Firefly's own model only (Firefly Image 5, or Image 4 Ultra if 5 is not offered). Never a partner model.
7. Every file gets a line in content/image-licences.ts (provider Adobe Firefly, the Firefly job reference, what it depicts, AI-generated, approver) before it is placed.

## Settings for every image

| Setting | Value |

| App | firefly.adobe.com, Text to Image |

| Transparency | generate on the white ground as written; the cut-out (alpha PNG or WebP) is made afterwards with Remove background in Adobe Express, or by us |
