# 3D visuals for the website: where, what and how many

Prepared 23 November 2026 from a full scan of the new website build (86 pages). For review before generation starts.

**Style for every item**: 3D rendered scene in the style of the current FMCG page illustration (soft studio light, clean isometric or three-quarter view, brand palette of navy, steel blue, light blue and sand), transparent background, no readable text, no logos. Each image is animated on the site by the scroll engine (slow float, gentle rotation, parallax, reveal as the visitor scrolls), so the pages feel alive without a video on every page.

**Policy note**: the interim image policy allows generated decoration but not generated depictions of our people, offices or clients. The current FMCG illustration shows two people who read as our staff. The list below keeps the same 3D scene style but with equipment, products and environments only, no people. If people are wanted in some scenes, that needs a written exception to the policy.

## 4. Totals and cost

| | Count |
|---|---|
| Videos | 4 (1 essential, 3 optional) |
| 3D images | 51 (42 objects and icons, 9 covers) |
| Pages that need nothing new | Legal, job openings, Events, Awards, Partnerships, Where we stand, and 6 service pages with explainers |

Decision: generate on Higgsfield, Plus plan (1,200 credits a month), one month, no auto-refill.

What that needs under the policy: a written line from the MD adding Higgsfield to the approved provider list, on the paid plan with the invoice kept. Inside Higgsfield, images on Nano Banana Pro or Seedream, and video on the Veo model only, because Kling, Seedance, Hailuo and Vidu are excluded by the policy. Provenance metadata stays in the files and every file gets a register entry before it goes live.

Credits: 51 images × 4 attempts on Nano Banana Pro about 410; hero video V1 on Veo 3.1, 5 takes, about 250; total about 660 of 1,200. The optional slide loops V2 to V5 would need a one-time top-up.

## 5. Order of work

1. Phase A: hero video V1, pillar objects 12 to 14, the three large Industries panels 25 to 27.
2. Phase B: service icons 4 to 10, service heroes 15 to 22, industry heroes 28 to 33, insight covers 34 to 37.
3. Phase C: hub loops V2 to V4, values 43 to 47, single objects 48 to 51, case-study covers 38 to 42.

Each generated file is recorded in the image register with provider, reference, date and approver before it goes live, as the policy requires. Review date for the whole set: 20 November 2027.
